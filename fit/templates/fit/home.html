Goal-Based Fitness Plans

Align your diet, caloric intake, and target training routines to your exact ambition.

Fat Loss Blueprint

Engineered to optimize lipid oxidation while preserving dense skeletal muscle tissue via an elegant negative energy balance.

Caloric & Water Strategy

Maintain a -500 kcal deficit. Drink 3.5L water daily to flush byproducts of lipid lipolysis.

Cardio Guidelines

150 mins of Zone 2 LISS weekly + 2 HIIT sessions (e.g. sprints) to spike EPOC.

Physiological Pro-Tip

"Consume high-volume, low-calorie foods. Fiber increases stomach distension, signaling satiety to the brain."

Fat Burning Food Directory

Proteins (Lean)

Chicken Breast, Turkey Breast, Egg Whites, Cod, Tilapia, Tuna, Whey Isolate, Low-Fat Cottage Cheese.

Carbohydrates (Fiber-Dense)

Rolled Oats, Quinoa, Sweet Potatoes, Cauliflower Rice, Broccoli, Spinach, Asparagus, Berries.

Fats (Essential)

Avocado, Raw Almonds, Chia Seeds, Extra Virgin Olive Oil (in restricted amounts).

🌙 Dinner

Bulking Blueprint

Formulated to maximize lean skeletal muscle mass development through a systematic calorie surplus paired with heavy compound progression lifts.

Caloric Strategy

Maintain a +300 to +500 kcal surplus above maintenance to stimulate anabolic signaling.

Mass Gain Workouts

Heavy compound lifts (Squat, Bench, Deadlift, Overhead Press) focused on progressive overload (3-5 mins rest between sets).

Physiological Pro-Tip

"Clean bulking is superior. Excess calorie surpluses above 500 kcal only convert to adipocytes, not additional myofibrillar proteins."

High-Calorie Mass Gain Foods

Proteins (Hormone-Supporting)

Whole Eggs, Ribeye Steak, Lean Ground Beef, Salmon, Paneer, Whole Milk, Chicken Thighs, Casein Protein.

Carbohydrates (Glycogen-Replenishing)

White Jasmine Rice, Whole Wheat Pasta, Granola, Bananas, Sweet Potato Mash, Oats, Bagels.

Fats (Calorie-Dense)

Natural Peanut Butter, Almond Butter, Avocados, Virgin Coconut Oil, Grass-Fed Butter, Walnuts, Cashews.

Cutting & Vascularity

Engineered to strip remaining stubborn fat reserves, optimize muscle hardness, and emphasize clean skeletal lines.

Carbohydrate Cycling

Restrict carbohydrates to workout windows. High carbs on leg days, low carbs on rest days.

Sodium & Fluid Management

Keep sodium intake stable but moderate. Drink 4L+ water to keep aldosterone levels low and prevent subcutaneous water retention.

Physiological Pro-Tip

"Ensure daily protein is elevated (2.2g+ per kg) during a cut. This prevents the body from oxidizing skeletal proteins for gluconeogenesis."

Lean & Definition Foods

Proteins (Leanest)

Turkey Breast, Tilapia, Shrimp, Egg Whites, Liquid Amino Acids, Lean Venison, Whey Isolate.

Carbohydrates (High-Fiber/Low-GI)

Cauliflower Rice, Broccoli, Zucchini Noodles, Asparagus, Cabbage, Quinoa (moderate), Cucumber, Celery.

Fats (Strictly Regulated)

MCT Oil, Chia Seeds, Omega-3 Fish Oil Capsules (capsule supplementation preferred over heavy fatty meats).

Sample Cutting Meal Schedule

🌅 Breakfast

Omelet made with 6 Egg Whites, half cup spinach, and mushrooms + 1 cup green tea (no sugar).

☀️ Lunch & Snack

Lunch: 180g Baked Turkey Breast + 2 cups Cauliflower Rice + steamed asparagus.
Snack: 1 scoop Whey Isolate in cold water + 1 Capsule Omega-3.

🌙 Dinner

180g grilled Shrimp served with large bowl of Zucchini Noodles (zoodles) tossed in garlic and 1 tsp MCT oil.

Healthy Weight Gain Blueprint

Optimized for ectomorphs ("hard gainers") struggling to build robust physical body weight. Focuses on digestible, calorie-dense foods to prevent gastrointestinal bloating.

Caloric Frequency

Eat every 3 hours. Consistency is paramount to keep insulin in a steady anabolic state.

Strength Focus

Prioritize strength routines with low rep ranges (4-8 reps) to build structural bone density and thick muscle tissue.

Physiological Pro-Tip

"If you struggle with high solid food volume, consume calorie-dense liquid shakes. Blending bananas, dates, oats, and peanut butter is highly digestible."

Calorie-Dense Weight Gain Foods

Proteins (Tissue-Building)

Full-fat Greek Yogurt, Salmon, Lean Beef, Paneer, Whole Milk, Chicken Thighs, Cheddar Cheese.

Carbohydrates (Calorie-Rich)

Granola, Medjool Dates, Raisins, Dried Figs, Sweet Potatoes, Whole Wheat Flatbreads, Oats, Honey.

Fats (Healthy Lipids)

Cashew Butter, Almond Butter, Extra Virgin Olive Oil, Organic Coconut Oil, Pumpkin Seeds, Avocados.
